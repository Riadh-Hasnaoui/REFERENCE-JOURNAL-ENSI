\section{Snowballing Protocol and Log}
\label{appendix:snowballing}

Following the guidelines of Wohlin~\cite{wohlin2014snowballing}, we applied both
\emph{backward} and \emph{forward} snowballing to the set of studies that survived
Phase-2 full-text screening (187 candidates before quality assessment).

\subsection*{Protocol}

Snowballing was executed in two rounds.  A \emph{round} consists of one iteration of
both backward and forward passes; subsequent rounds start from the newly added studies.
The procedure terminates when no new studies are added.

\paragraph{Backward Snowballing.}
The reference list of each Phase-2 survivor was examined.  Every referenced paper that
(i) was not already in the candidate pool, (ii) satisfied all IC/EC criteria on
title-and-abstract screening, and (iii) was accessible in full text was added to a
``snowball queue'' for full-text assessment.

\paragraph{Forward Snowballing.}
The Semantic Scholar Citation API was queried for each Phase-2 survivor.  Papers that
cited any survivor and that were not already in the pool were subjected to title-and-abstract
screening; those passing were added to the queue.

\subsection*{Results}

Two complete rounds were performed.  Round 1 identified 24 new candidates, of which
21 passed full-text screening.  Round 2 identified 9 new candidates, of which 6 passed.
Round 3 found no new candidates; snowballing therefore terminated after two rounds.
In total, snowballing contributed \textbf{27 additional primary studies} to the final
corpus of 160 studies (17\% of the total).

\begin{table}[h]
\caption{Summary of snowballing rounds (Wohlin~\cite{wohlin2014snowballing} procedure).}
\label{tab:snowballing-rounds}
\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
\textbf{Round} & \textbf{Direction} & \textbf{Candidates found} & \textbf{Passed screening} & \textbf{Included after QA} \\
\midrule
1 & Backward  & 16 & 13 & 13 \\
1 & Forward   &  8 &  8 &  8 \\
2 & Backward  &  5 &  3 &  3 \\
2 & Forward   &  4 &  3 &  3 \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\noindent The 27 studies added through snowballing are identified by ``(S)'' in the
replication-package spreadsheet.  Representative additions include
RepoHyper~\cite{phan2025repohyper}, GRACE~\cite{wang2025grace},
RepoScope~\cite{liu2025reposcope}, and
ArtifactSync~\cite{alor2026artifactsync}---all of which would have been missed by the
primary database search because they were either very recent preprints or used
non-standard terminology in their abstracts.
