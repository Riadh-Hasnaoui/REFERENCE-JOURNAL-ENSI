\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

This SLR has characterised seven paradigms through which LLM-based systems
represent code repositories, assessed their technology readiness levels across
six task objectives, and systematically identified five research gaps (RG-1
through RG-5) with a concrete Research Agenda.  The most critical
near-term priorities are: (i)~advancing PAR-4 (execution-based
representation) beyond proof-of-concept; (ii)~developing cross-session memory
architectures for long-horizon coding; (iii)~constructing benchmarks for
under-served tasks (O5 refactoring, O6 test generation); (iv)~adopting
temporally-faithful evaluation protocols to eliminate stale-context bias; and
(v)~building a cross-paradigm benchmark to enable evidence-based paradigm
selection in practice.
