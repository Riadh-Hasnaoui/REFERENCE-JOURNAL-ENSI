\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:relatedwork}

Several surveys cover LLMs for software engineering broadly.
Hou et al.~\cite{hou2024llmse_slr} survey LLM applications across the full
SE lifecycle but do not isolate repository representation strategies.
Husein et al.~\cite{husein2025llmcompletion_slr} focus on code completion
but limit their scope to single-file methods.
Jiang et al.~\cite{jiang2026surveycodegen} cover code-generation techniques
without a dedicated taxonomy of context representation.
Tao et al.~\cite{tao2026ragsurvey} survey RAG-for-code but cover only PAR-2
(retrieval-based) approaches.
Zhang et al.~\cite{zhang2026surveyse} provide a broad SE survey but pre-date
the agentic wave (PAR-7).

Our work differs in three ways: (i)~it covers all seven paradigms under a
unified framework; (ii)~it provides the first systematic trade-off analysis
across the five operational dimensions; and (iii)~all supporting data are
publicly archived in a replication package.
