\section{Results: RQ1--RQ3}\label{sec:results}

\subsection{RQ1 --- Paradigm Distribution}\label{sec:rq1}

Across $N=163$ primary studies, PAR-2 (Cross-File RAG) is the dominant
paradigm with $n=52$ studies (31.9\%), followed by PAR-3 (Graph-Based,
$n=31$, 19.0\%) and PAR-7 (Agentic, $n=28$, 17.2\%).  PAR-4
(Execution-Based) is the most under-represented with $n=7$ studies (4.3\%).

\subsection{RQ2 --- Task Objectives and Benchmarks}

Task Objective O1 (Code Completion) and O4 (Issue/Bug Resolution) account for
74\% of primary studies.  O5 (Refactoring) and O6 (Test Generation) each
represent fewer than 5\% of the corpus.  SWE-bench~\cite{jimenez2024swebench}
and RepoBench~\cite{liu2024repobench} are the two most frequently employed
benchmarks.

\subsection{RQ3 --- Technology Readiness Levels}\label{sec:rq3}

PAR-2 and PAR-7 show the highest industrial readiness: 14\% and 9\% of
studies respectively achieve TRL-C (industrial deployment).  PAR-4 reaches
only TRL-A (proof-of-concept, 43\%), and PAR-5 achieves \textbf{0\%} at
TRL-C.
