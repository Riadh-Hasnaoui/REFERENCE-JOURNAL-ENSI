\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\paragraph{Internal Validity.}
The inclusion/exclusion criteria were applied independently by two reviewers
($\kappa = 0.81$); however, subjective judgements in borderline cases cannot
be fully eliminated.  We mitigated this by requiring consensus resolution for
all conflicts.

\paragraph{External Validity.}
The corpus is limited to papers published in 2020–2025 in English.  Relevant
grey literature or non-English publications may have been missed.  The
snowballing procedure (Wohlin 2014~\cite{wohlin2014snowballing}) partially
compensates by following citation chains regardless of venue.

\paragraph{Construct Validity.}
Our seven-class taxonomy reflects themes identified through open coding.
Alternative taxonomies are plausible; we address this by making all codes
and extraction data available in the replication package (Zenodo).

\paragraph{Conclusion Validity.}
Quantitative claims (e.g., percentage of corpus per paradigm) are derived
from the extraction spreadsheets archived in the replication package and are
therefore reproducible.
