\section{Quality Assessment Scores}
\label{appendix:qa}

Table~\ref{tab:qa-scores} presents the aggregated quality assessment scores across the eight
QA dimensions for the 160 primary studies. The first column gives the QA question identifier,
the second the mean score across all studies, the third the standard deviation, and the fourth
the fraction of studies that scored 1 (full credit) on that question.

\begin{table}[h]
\caption{Aggregated quality assessment scores (N=160).}
\label{tab:qa-scores}
\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
\textbf{QA Dimension} & \textbf{Mean} & \textbf{SD} & \textbf{Score=1 (\%)} \\
\midrule
QA1: RQ clarity         & 0.91 & 0.22 & 89.4 \\
QA2: PAR description    & 0.82 & 0.30 & 78.8 \\
QA3: Benchmark public   & 0.74 & 0.35 & 68.1 \\
QA4: Metrics approp.    & 0.88 & 0.24 & 85.6 \\
QA5: Baseline comp.     & 0.85 & 0.27 & 81.9 \\
QA6: Threats validity   & 0.61 & 0.39 & 51.9 \\
QA7: Implementation     & 0.58 & 0.41 & 48.1 \\
QA8: Conclusions valid  & 0.87 & 0.26 & 83.8 \\
\midrule
\textbf{Overall}        & \textbf{6.26} & \textbf{1.14} & -- \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The weakest dimensions are QA6 (Threats to validity, 62\% of maximum) and QA7 (Implementation
availability, 58\% of maximum). The low score on QA7 is consistent with the finding noted in
Section~\ref{subsec:rq5} that approximately 22\% of systems do not release code artifacts.
The low score on QA6 reflects a broader tendency in the LLM-based software engineering
community to under-report validity threats---a pattern that has been observed in other
systematic reviews of this field.
