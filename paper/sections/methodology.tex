\section{Research Methodology}\label{sec:methodology}

We follow the SLR guidelines of Kitchenham~\cite{kitchenham2007guidelines},
augmented with backward/forward snowballing per Wohlin~\cite{wohlin2014snowballing}
and reported according to PRISMA~2020~\cite{prisma2020}.  Thematic synthesis
follows the protocol of Cruzes~\&~Menzies~\cite{cruzes2011thematic}, and
quality assessment applies the ACM Empirical Standards~\cite{acm_empirical_standards}.
